All files are byte-identical to the supplied original artwork. Original
proportions and complete lockups are retained on the page's shared background.
KAI, MathWorks and Ansys switch between their supplied light/dark variants.
The MATLAB entry now uses the full MathWorks logo, including its colored
membrane and registered wordmark, rather than a fabricated combination.
The earlier text-only MATLAB asset remains archived but is not rendered.
No color filters, tracing or cropping are applied to supporter artwork.
KAI's identity and supporter relationship were confirmed by the site owner
on 28 September 2026; this does not assert a particular sponsorship tier.
The department uses the POSTECH wordmark. Its separate text label identifies
the department and is not presented as an official combined logo.
The website uses the 733 × 62 official POSTECH file for both POSTECH entries
to retain sharp edges on high-DPI displays. The older 236 × 22 department
download remains unchanged in the source archive but is no longer rendered.

MATLAB and MathWorks are registered trademarks of The MathWorks, Inc. MATLAB

the complete “part of Synopsys” lockup and its trademark marks. POSTECH and
Ansys/Synopsys marks remain the property of their respective owners. Artwork
is used for supporter identification and is not licensed as PSI artwork.
