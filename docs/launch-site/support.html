Starting a conversation

Each proposal is discussed with the team. No arrangement is assumed before PSI and the supporter agree on its scope.

  1. Write to PSITell us what kind of support or question you have.
  2. Discuss the scopeWe can discuss needs, timing and practical terms.
  3. Agree on an arrangementAny support proceeds only after both sides agree.
